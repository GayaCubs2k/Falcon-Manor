    Falcon Manor HOA & Tax Rates

    HOA fees & property tax information

    Falcon Manor is designed to provide a well-maintained residential environment with attractive landscaping, community amenities, and common areas. The Homeowners Association (HOA) is responsible for maintaining designated common areas and preserving the overall appearance and quality of the community.

    $1,500Annual HOA assessment per homesite

    Homeowners Association (HOA) fees

    HOA fees for Falcon Manor
    Fee descriptionAmount
    Annual HOA Assessment$1,500 per homesite
    One-Time Capitalization Fee$1,500
    HOA Transfer Fee$300 per ownership transfer
    Resale Certificate FeeAs determined by the HOA management company

    Annual HOA Assessment: The $1,500 annual assessment supports the ongoing maintenance and management of community common areas, landscaping, entrance features, lakes, trails, and other shared amenities, as applicable.

    Capitalization Fee: A one-time $1,500 contribution collected at the initial home purchase to help establish HOA operating reserves and support community maintenance needs.

    Transfer and Resale Fees: A $300 HOA transfer fee and applicable resale certificate fees may be assessed during subsequent property ownership transfers.

    Property tax information

    Falcon Manor is located in Waller County, Texas. Property owners are subject to property taxes assessed by the applicable local taxing authorities.

    Property tax rates for Falcon Manor

    Tax rates are expressed as percentages of taxable property value and are based on the provided Waller County tax records. Rates are subject to change.

    Municipal Utility District (MUD)

    Falcon Manor is located within Harris-Waller Counties Municipal Utility District No. 13 (MUD 13). The MUD provides a financing mechanism for eligible public infrastructure and utility improvements serving the community.

    The MUD tax rate of 1.50% is already included in the total combined property tax rate of 3.211266% shown above and is not an additional tax.

    Understanding your property taxes

    Property taxes are generally calculated by applying the combined tax rate to the property's taxable assessed value, after applicable exemptions.

    Illustrative estimates use a combined tax rate of 3.211266%, assume the stated taxable value after any exemptions, and exclude HOA assessments and other applicable charges.

    • HOA assessments and fees are subject to the community's governing documents and may be adjusted from time to time.
    • Property tax rates are based on the supplied Waller County tax information and are provided for general informational purposes only. Actual taxes may vary based on property valuations, exemptions, changes in taxing authority rates, and other applicable factors.
    • Prospective buyers are encouraged to independently verify current tax rates, HOA fees, MUD information, and applicable assessments with the appropriate taxing authorities and HOA management company before purchasing a homesite or home.

    Resident questions

    How much is the annual HOA assessment?

    The annual HOA assessment is $1,500 per homesite. It supports the ongoing maintenance and management of community common areas, landscaping, entrance features, lakes, trails, and other shared amenities, as applicable.

    What is the capitalization fee?

    A one-time $1,500 contribution collected at the initial home purchase to help establish HOA operating reserves and support community maintenance needs.

    Are there fees when a home is resold?

    A $300 HOA transfer fee and applicable resale certificate fees may be assessed during subsequent property ownership transfers. The resale certificate fee is determined by the HOA management company.

    What is the property tax rate?

    The total combined property tax rate is 3.211266%, based on the provided Waller County tax records. Rates are subject to change.

    What is the MUD, and is it an extra tax?

    Falcon Manor is located within Harris-Waller Counties Municipal Utility District No. 13. The MUD tax rate of 1.50% is already included in the total combined property tax rate of 3.211266% and is not an additional tax.

    Are the amenities open yet?

    The amenity lakes are proposed, and the walking trails and recreation site are planned. They are not yet built.
